## 🧩 Problem Description

Given an integer array `nums`, find three numbers whose product is maximum and return the maximum product.

---

## 📌 Examples

**Example 1:**
- **Input:** `nums` = `[1, 2, 3]`
- **Output:** `6`
- **Explanation:** The only three numbers in the array are `1`, `2`, and `3`. Their product is $1 \times 2 \times 3 = 6$.

**Example 2:**
- **Input:** `nums` = `[1, 2, 3, 4]`
- **Output:** `24`
- **Explanation:** The three numbers whose product is maximum are `2`, `3`, and `4`. Their product is $2 \times 3 \times 4 = 24$.

**Example 3:**
- **Input:** `nums` = `[-1, -2, -3]`
- **Output:** `-6`
- **Explanation:** The only three numbers in the array are `-1`, `-2`, and `-3`. Their product is $(-1) \times (-2) \times (-3) = -6$.

---

## 📐 Constraints
- $3 \le \text{nums.length} \le 10^4$
- $-1000 \le \text{nums}[i] \le 1000$
